The rapid growth of digital technologies and the proliferation of connected devices have led to an explosion of data in all domains of human activity. This enormous and continuously expanding volume of information, known as Big Data, has become one of the most significant assets of the modern world. Big data refers not only to the vast amount of data, but also to its high velocity, wide variety, and varying veracity. These characteristics challenge traditional data management systems and demand new analytical tools and computational frameworks. The study of Big Data encompasses techniques for collecting, storing, processing, and analysing massive datasets to uncover hidden patterns, trends, and correlations that can guide intelligent decision making. With the advent of distributed computing models such as Hadoop and Spark, it has become possible to handle data at scales once thought impossible. This project aims to provide a foundational understanding of Big Data concepts, its architecture, technologies, and applications, highlighting how efficient data analysis can shape innovation and improve the quality of life in an increasingly data-driven society.
 
\textit{\textbf{Keywords:}} Big Data, Digital Technologies, intelligent Decision-making, Quality of life
